General information

Hours and services

Monday to Friday, 8:00 am to 5:00 pm. There is a 15-minute grace period for rush-hour traffic and emergencies after 5:00; after that a late fee of $1.00 a minute is added to the following week’s fees.

  • Full-time: Monday to Friday. Four days a week counts as full-time.
  • Part-time: three days a week.
  • Short term: spring break, summer break and emergency drop-off.
  • We serve children from 3 months to 12 years, support exclusive breastfeeding, potty train, fully prepare children for kindergarten, and serve home-cooked breakfast and lunch.
Activities

ABCs, 123s, reading, colors and shapes, basic writing, arts and crafts, cooking, indoor and outdoor play, prayers, Christian music, Bible verses, 15 to 20 minutes of TV when needed (PBS Kids, Mother Goose Club, Christian music), and short trips round town: the Discovery Museum, the library, trails and parks.

Drop-off and pickup

Only the people listed on your child’s record, or someone with written permission from a parent, may sign your child in or out. In the portal you can add authorized pickups yourself, and staff see a photo ID reminder at check-out.

Childcare assistance

When the provider is away, her assistant, Thomas Anokye-Gyambrah, is always available to help, on the phone or in person.

Photographs

Photos of children at play and on field trips are taken from time to time, shared with parents, used in crafts, and may appear on our Facebook page and website. If you would rather your child is not photographed, tell us in writing (or switch it off in the portal).

Filing your taxes

We keep track of every payment you make. At the end of the year you get a statement with the total you paid and our tax ID, ready for your return. Please don’t add payments made to another provider under our tax ID.

Fees and payments

Tuition

Please call for current tuition. We accept Zelle, Venmo, check or cash. Allkidz may change tuition, and parents will be told as soon as rates change.

  • A non-refundable registration fee holds your child’s spot and is paid before care begins.
  • Payment is due on the Friday before the week of care.
  • A $10 fee is added for each day payment is late. After two weeks late, the fee is $20 a day.
  • There is a $30 fee for a bounced check. After a second bounced check, we accept cash only.
  • Care may end if payment keeps being a problem.
When we are closed
  • If Allkidz is closed for a holiday, or for one or two days in a week, the weekly payment is still due in full. If your child attends at least four days, the full rate is always due.
  • If Allkidz is closed three days or more in a week, the weekly rate is prorated.
  • Families pay the full weekly fee during family vacations to hold their space. Please give two weeks’ notice of a vacation.
  • If your child is out sick, payment is due in full for that week.
  • Families on a school-year calendar may break the contract for summer break.
  • If Allkidz must close for health and safety, such as a deep clean after an outbreak, payment for that week is due in full. For closures over a week ordered by the Virginia Department of Health, we will talk about payment together.

Closings and holidays

Paid holidays

Allkidz closes for New Year’s Eve and Day, Martin Luther King Jr. Day, Good Friday and Easter Monday, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day and the Friday after, and Christmas Eve and Christmas Day. See the calendar for this year’s dates.

Provider’s vacation

The provider takes up to three weeks of vacation each year, taken in parts. You will always be told ahead of time so you can arrange care. You can pay a little extra each week to cover vacation weeks, or pay the day before the vacation.

Health and sickness

Please keep sick children at home

Please call or message by 8 am if your child will not be in that day (or report the absence in the portal). We do not give medication, so please give any medicine before drop-off.

  • A child with a fever of 100°F or higher cannot attend, and must be fever-free for 24 hours without fever reducers before coming back.
  • If we call you because your child is sick, please pick up within the hour.
  • A child with a contagious illness may return only with a doctor’s note and the provider’s OK.
  • Tell us if your child was sick over the weekend, so we can watch the other children.
Reasons a child must stay home
  • Fever of 100°F or higher, vomiting, persistent diarrhea
  • Cold with yellow or green nasal discharge, constant clear runny nose, productive or persistent phlegmy cough
  • Croup, strep throat, flu, stomach flu
  • Ringworm, chicken pox, hand-foot-and-mouth, coxsackie, fifth disease, mumps, impetigo, pink eye, head lice, MRSA, scabies
  • Any rash or blisters
Health checks at the door

Each morning we check temperatures and ask a few health questions at check-in. Shoes come off at the door, because we are all crawlers here, and hand sanitizer is by the entrance for signing in and out. Hard floors are sanitized daily and carpets weekly.

Hygiene

Arriving ready for the day
  • Children arrive bathed, in a clean diaper and daytime clothes. No pajamas, except babies under 12 months.
  • Shoes come off on arrival, so please make sure feet are clean.
  • Please keep fingernails trimmed.
  • Pack extra clothes in the backpack, and label everything, especially socks and underwear.
  • Bring a special blanket, crib sheet or pillow if it helps your child nap.

Meals and snacks

Home-cooked, healthy food

We provide breakfast, lunch and a snack. Please arrive between 7:30 and 8:00 to enjoy breakfast, or feed your child at home if you will arrive later. The weekly menu is in the portal and on the Our Day page.

  • Bring a sippy cup or water bottle.
  • Formula, specific baby food and special diets come from home, labelled.
  • Please don’t send food, drinks or candy unless it is for everyone to share.
  • Tell us about every allergy in advance.

Rest and safe sleep

Rest time

Every child lies down to rest after lunch. Infants and young toddlers have their own pack-n-play; older toddlers and preschoolers have a mat or flip-open bed. A pillow, blanket and soft friend can stay at Allkidz.

Safe sleep, at Allkidz and at home
  • Put baby on their back for every sleep, in a crib or pack-n-play, on a firm mattress with a fitted sheet.
  • No blankets, pillows, toys or bumpers in the crib. Use a wearable blanket for warmth.
  • Never put a baby to sleep on a couch, armchair, adult bed, car seat, swing or stroller.
  • Stop swaddling once baby shows signs of rolling, and don’t let baby overheat.

Breastfeeding

We support nursing mothers

Mothers have a private, clean place to nurse or express milk, with a comfortable chair, an outlet and running water nearby, and a fridge for expressed milk in labelled containers. We hold off on a bottle when mom is due, babies are always held for feeding and bottles are never propped. Formula and solids are given only when mom asks.

Outdoor play

Outside every day

Weather permitting, children take a supervised morning walk and play outside daily: 60 to 90 minutes for 12 months to 3 years, 90 to 120 minutes for 3 and up, and supervised floor play for babies. Swings, slides, balls, basketball, soccer, bikes, sand and, in summer, swimming.

Please send weather-ready clothes, plus sunscreen, a hat and insect repellent. Parents are always welcome to join.

Potty training

When they’re ready, as a team

We potty train at whatever age your child seems ready, working together with you at home. Signs of readiness: staying dry longer, interest in the potty, telling you they need to go, and enjoying it.

  • During training, keep sending diapers.
  • Once your child is using the potty with few accidents, switch to pull-ups.
  • Easy pull-up-and-down clothes help a lot.

House rules and discipline

How we guide behavior

We ask children not to hit, push, bite, kick, scratch, pinch, name-call, tease or mistreat toys. We never spank or use demeaning words. We redirect, teach children to use their words, and use positive reinforcement and rewards; occasionally a short time-out or losing a toy for a while. The first two weeks are a settling-in trial. A child who keeps hurting others cannot stay, so that every child goes home safe.

Supply list

For your child’s cubby
  • A complete change of clothes (more during potty training): shirt, pants, socks and underwear
  • Outerwear for outside play
  • Two blankets, or a sheet and a blanket, and a pillow
  • Insect repellent; swimsuit, towel and sunscreen in summer
  • Not yet potty trained: a full pack of diapers or pull-ups, a box of 80 to 100 wipes, two changes of clothes, and any diaper cream

Everything labelled with your child’s name. The portal reminds you when supplies run low.

Termination

Ending care

Parents and provider agree to give two weeks’ written notice, starting the first Monday after notice is received; two weeks’ fees are due in one payment at that time. The provider may end care immediately for non-compliance with the handbook, non-payment, late payments, bounced checks, lack of cooperation, disrespect, missing forms, abuse of any person or property, serious illness in the provider’s family, continuing discipline problems or false information.
