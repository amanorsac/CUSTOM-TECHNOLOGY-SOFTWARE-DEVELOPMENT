Why families choose Allkidz

A home. A school. A family.

The care of a family home with the structure of a preschool, and the Word of God at the center of every day.

Faith at the center

Morning prayers, a Bible story and memory verse every day, and Christian songs all through it.

Kindergarten ready

ABCs, 123s, colors, shapes, reading, first writing, arts and crafts and cooking.

Home-cooked meals

Breakfast, lunch and an afternoon snack, cooked fresh in the Allkidz kitchen.

Outside every day

An hour to two hours of outdoor play a day depending on age, plus trips to the library, parks and the Discovery Museum.

Photos all day

Pictures, videos and a daily report in the parent portal, so you can be at peace at work.

Licensed and trained

State licensed, a CDA credential, CPR and First Aid, and background checks for every adult in the home.

All year round

Families welcome.

Parent meetings in January, a family get-together in June and a Christmas party every December.

Meet Miss Hannah

A teacher, a mom and a children’s minister.

Hannah Anokye-Gyambrah taught in public school in Ghana for fourteen years before moving to Charlottesville in 2012. Caring for her own newborn twins led her to open Allkidz in her home in 2015.

  • B.Ed in Basic and Elementary Education
  • Child Development Associate (CDA) credential
  • Virginia state licensed family day home
  • CPR, First Aid and Medication Administration trained
  • Board member, Virginia Association for the Education of Young Children (Central Chapter)
