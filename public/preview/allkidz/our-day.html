Outdoors

Outside, every single day.

An hour to an hour and a half for toddlers, an hour and a half to two hours for threes and up. Babies get supervised floor play.

Outdoor policy

This week’s menu

Cooked fresh, eaten together.

Breakfast, lunch and an afternoon snack, cooked in the Allkidz kitchen. Parents see the menu here and in the portal every week.

Tell us about allergies when you register. Formula, baby food and special diets come from home, labelled with your child’s name.

What we learn

Learning through play.

Children explore at their own pace with hands-on materials kept at their level, and we put their work on the walls.

Letters and numbers

ABCs, 123s, colors, shapes, reading and basic writing.

Bible and prayer

Bible stories, memory verses, prayers and Christian music.

Arts, crafts and cooking

Painting, gluing, play dough and helping in the kitchen.

Music and movement

Songs, rhymes and dancing at circle time.

Trips round town

The library, parks, trails and the Discovery Museum.

A little screen time

Fifteen to twenty minutes when needed: PBS Kids and Christian music.

Rest and safe sleep

Rested children have happier evenings.

After lunch everyone lies down, even the older children. Infants and young toddlers sleep in their own pack-n-play; older toddlers and preschoolers get a mat or flip-open bed.

  • Babies always sleep on their backs, alone in their crib, watched by an adult
  • A small pillow, blanket and soft friend can stay at Allkidz
  • Nap times are logged in the parent portal
