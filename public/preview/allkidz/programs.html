Infants · from 3 months

Babies, held close.

Your baby keeps their own rhythm for feeding and sleeping. Bottles are always held, never propped, and every nap is on their back in their own crib or pack-n-play.

  • Safe-sleep practice for every nap, with an adult watching
  • Breastfeeding supported: a private, comfortable place to nurse, and a fridge for labelled milk
  • Supervised floor play and tummy time in a safe space
  • Feeds, naps and diapers logged in your parent portal as they happen
Our breastfeeding policy

Toddlers · 16 months to 3

Busy hands, big hearts.

Toddlers learn by doing: stacking, pouring, singing, climbing and sharing. We teach them to use their words with each other, and we celebrate every one.

  • Potty training when your child is ready, as a team with you at home
  • Songs, rhymes, music and movement every morning
  • An hour to an hour and a half outside each day
  • Positive, gentle guidance. Never spanking or harsh words

Preschool · 3 to 5

Ready for kindergarten.

Every morning has a learning block: ABCs, 123s, colors, shapes, reading and first writing, plus arts and crafts, cooking and a Bible memory verse. By kindergarten, they are ready.

  • A weekly theme and lesson plan, shared with parents
  • Library story time, parks, trails and the Discovery Museum

  • Their work on the walls, so they see how proud we are

Questions parents ask

Good to know.

What ages do you take?

Children from 3 months to 12 years. Allkidz is a family home childcare and preschool, so babies, toddlers, preschoolers and school-age children share one home and siblings stay together.

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday, 8:00 am to 5:00 pm. There is a 15-minute grace period at pickup for traffic and emergencies; after that a late fee of $1 a minute is added to the next week’s bill.

Do you provide food?

Yes. Home-cooked breakfast, lunch and an afternoon snack are included. Please tell us about allergies when you register. Formula, specific baby food and special diets come from home.

Do you help with potty training?

Yes, when your child shows they are ready. We work as a team with you at home, starting in diapers and moving to pull-ups once your child is regularly using the potty.

Is Allkidz licensed?

Yes. Allkidz is a Virginia state licensed family day home. Miss Hannah holds a CDA credential and a B.Ed, keeps CPR and First Aid current, and every adult in the home has a background check, renewed every five years.

How do I pay?

Weekly, by the Friday before the week of care. We accept Zelle, Venmo, check or cash, and you can see every bill and payment in the parent portal. At the end of the year you get a statement for your taxes.
