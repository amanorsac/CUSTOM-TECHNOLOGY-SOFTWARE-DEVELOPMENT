Meet Miss Hannah.

Director and owner

Hannah Anokye-Gyambrah

Hannah taught in public school from 1998 to 2012, beginning her journey in Ghana, West Africa, before moving to the United States in March 2012. She is married to Apostle Dr. Thomas Anokye-Gyambrah, and God has blessed them with four children.

Caring for her newborn twins in 2012 and working as a nanny, she felt a growing desire to care for children in her home. A deep search of the Bible about child development and parenting made it stronger, and in October 2015 she opened Allkidz.

She serves as children’s minister at Christ the King Church in Charlottesville, which she co-founded, along with Christ the King Bible Seminary.

Our mission

A home-like place, in the fear of the Lord.

Our mission is to provide a home-like atmosphere for all children in the fear of the Lord, and to help them grow in the wisdom of God, through Bible stories and Christian songs every day.

Every child is different

Each one is fearfully and wonderfully made, whatever their background or behavior (Psalm 139:14).

The whole child

We help each child grow cognitively, physically, emotionally, socially and spiritually.

At their own pace

Children explore by tasting, hearing, smelling, touching and seeing, with materials at their reach.

Joy every day

Hugs and songs at the door, laughing together, eating together, and correcting with love.

Partners with parents

Pictures and videos through the day, so you can be at peace at work.

Always learning

Everyone who helps at Allkidz keeps training, taking classes and studying.

A family ministry

Two pairs of hands, always.

Allkidz is a family home childcare and preschool. When Miss Hannah is away, her husband and assistant, Thomas Anokye-Gyambrah, is always available to help, on the phone or in person.

Every adult in the home has completed a criminal background check, renewed every five years.

Statement of faith

What we believe.

Allkidz is a Christian ministry. These are the beliefs that shape how we care for your children.

The Bible

We believe the Bible, Old and New Testament, is the verbally inspired Word of God, infallible, and the final authority for Christian faith and practice (2 Tim. 3:16; 2 Peter 1:20–21).

Creation

We believe God created the universe and all living things, and that all human beings are created by God (Gen. 1:1–2:3; 1:26–28).

The Trinity and God

We believe in one eternal God, sovereign and unchangeable, who manifests Himself in three persons: Father, Son and Holy Spirit (Matt. 28:19; 2 Cor. 13:14).

Jesus Christ

He is the Son of the Living God, conceived of the Holy Spirit and born of a virgin, fully God and fully man. He lived a sinless life, died for the sins of humanity, rose from the dead, and is coming again (Matt. 1:20–21; Luke 1:31–35).

The Holy Spirit

The third person of the Godhead, who convicts, regenerates, indwells and seals believers, gives power to live and witness, and gives the spiritual gifts, which are all for today (Rom. 12; 1 Cor. 12, 14).

Sin, redemption and salvation

We believe all have sinned and fall short of the glory of God; that Christ died for our sins; and that salvation is by grace through faith, the gift of God (Rom. 3:23; 1 Cor. 15:3; Eph. 2:8).

Biblical parenting

We believe the Word of God is sufficient for parenting children in the fear of the Lord (Deut. 6:1–9; Prov. 22:6).
