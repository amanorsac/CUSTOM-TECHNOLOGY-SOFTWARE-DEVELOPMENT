Her story

Three chapters so far

Chapter one

A career built on trust

Kezia holds the CISM, the Certified Information Security Manager credential. It reflects a career spent helping organizations protect the information, people and reputation they depend on.

Chapter two

A craving that became a franchise

The idea began with a craving during her pregnancy. On August 26, 2026, she opened Bonchon Korean Fried Chicken on West Main Street in Charlottesville, the brand's 500th location in the world.

Chapter three

Building something that lasts

She is also a Director of Kester International School, which teaches children from preschool through junior high. Three fields, one thread: building things that serve people well. Now she shares what she has learned along the way.
