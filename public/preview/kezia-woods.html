Chapter 01 · Ghana

Roots in Ghana

Kezia Woods was born in Ghana, and her life so far runs between two places: Ghana, where she helps lead a growing school, and Charlottesville, Virginia, where she now runs a restaurant on one of the city's busiest streets.

Editor's note: Kezia's own account of growing up, and what took her abroad, goes here.

Chapter 02 · Security

The security career

Before restaurants, there was risk. Kezia holds the CISM, ISACA's Certified Information Security Manager credential, earned by people who design and run information security programs: governance, risk, incident response.

It is a discipline built on asking what could go wrong before it does, and it shows up in everything else she builds.

A security mind, a teacher's patience, a restaurateur's timing.

Editor's note: her roles and organizations in security, from her own CV.

Chapter 03 · Tuba, Ga South

Kester International School

In Tuba, in Ghana's Ga South municipality, Kester International School teaches children from preschool through junior high, with science and ICT labs, a basketball court and its own transport.

Kezia is one of the school's three directors.

Editor's note: how she came to the school, and what she wants for its students.

Chapter 04 · Charlottesville

The craving

The next venture started with an appetite. While she was pregnant, Kezia found herself craving Bonchon's Korean fried chicken: hand-battered, fried twice, brushed with sauce by hand. Charlottesville didn't have one.

So she set out to bring it there, as a franchisee.

Chapter 05 · West Main Street

The 500th

On August 26, 2026, Bonchon opened on West Main Street. It was the chain's 500th restaurant in the world.

“I’m beyond blessed to have this opportunity.”
