Kingsley Anyenor Ministries

Revived to revive

The Refined is a non-denominational music and outreach group founded and led by Kingsley Anyenor in Accra, Ghana. Rooted in Malachi 3:1–3, it is dedicated to “revealing the glorified Christ in my generation”.

Malachi 3:1–3

The story

A Ghanaian gospel minister, worship leader and songwriter

Kingsley Anyenor is a Ghanaian gospel minister, worship leader, and songwriter based in Accra, Ghana. He is best known for his extended live worship sessions, particularly his ongoing Deeper Worship series.

Kingsley Anyenor Ministries operates primarily through The Refined, with the guiding motto “Revived to revive”.

The ministry

Three pillars

  1. 01

    Revival

    Igniting a fresh hunger for God's presence in churches, campuses, and communities.

  2. 02

    Consecration

    Calling believers back to a life of holiness, intimacy, and wholehearted surrender to Christ.

  3. 03

    Restoration

    Ministering healing, hope, and spiritual renewal through the prophetic power of worship and the Word.

Worship philosophy

Worship is a natural response

Worship is a “natural response to the greatness of God”.

Anyenor defines worship as a “natural response to the greatness of God” rather than mere words or musical tempo. His approach emphasizes a genuine heart posture, reconciliation, and leading believers into extended moments of prayer and spiritual reflection.
