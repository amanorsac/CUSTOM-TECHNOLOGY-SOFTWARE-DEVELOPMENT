This Sunday · Romans · Week 6

Grace That Holds

Pastor Dana Ellis · 38 min

0:00 / 38:00

When everything else gives way, grace does not. It is not a reward for holding on. It is the hand that holds on to you.

Paul writes to people who were tired, divided and unsure. He does not tell them to try harder. He tells them who is holding them.

So this week, set the weight down. Let grace carry what you were never meant to carry alone.
