Why we collect it

To reply to you and to prepare a proposal or a homepage mockup. We do not use it for anything else.

Who handles it and where it is stored

  • Supabase stores your submission in a database, and your logo file in file storage.
  • Resend sends the email notification to us and a short confirmation to you.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile protects the forms from spam. It checks your browser and IP address to tell people from bots.
  • Cloudflare hosts this site.
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics counts visits. It is cookieless and does not track you across other sites.

Cookies and browser storage

We do not set marketing cookies. The live demo keeps any changes you make only in your own browser session storage. They are not sent to us and disappear when you close the tab.

How long we keep it

We keep submissions for up to 24 months, then delete them. To have yours deleted sooner, email amanorsac@gmail.com and we will remove it.

No selling of data

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it except with the services listed above, which we need in order to run the site.

Children

This site is for businesses and organizations. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
