Our story

A custom technology partner, not just a web designer.

A website is often the public face of a project. The larger value is what happens behind it: customer registration, a CRM, a client portal, payments, automatic notifications, an admin dashboard and reporting.

CTSD, short for Custom Technology & Software Development, designs and builds all of it as one connected system, for businesses, churches, schools and nonprofits that have outgrown one-size-fits-all software.

Tell us what your organization needs to do. We will build the technology around it.

What makes us different

Five things we do differently.

  • Fully custom

    Every organization works differently. We design around your actual workflow instead of forcing you into a rigid template.

  • Front-end and back-end

    We build what your customers see and the systems behind it: databases, admin tools, automation, permissions and integrations.

  • Easy to manage

    You get a simple admin area for routine work like editing pages, adding events or products and reviewing submissions, so you are not calling a developer for every change.

  • Connected systems

    Website, app, CRM, portal, payments and automation work together as one system instead of five separate tools.

  • Flexible integrations

    Where custom does not make sense, we connect established platforms such as Stripe, HubSpot, Salesforce and Google Workspace.
