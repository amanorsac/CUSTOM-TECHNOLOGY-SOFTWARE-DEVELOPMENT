# CTSD — Brand Identity Document

*Source: provided by the owner on 2026-10-03. Copy and voice come from here. Colors and fonts are overridden by the brand board (`brand-board.webp`); see spec §5.*

## 1. Brand overview
- **Brand name:** Custom Technology & Software Development
- **Short name:** CTSD
- **Primary tagline:** Technology built around your organization.
- **Secondary tagline:** Websites. Apps. Systems. Built for the way you work.
- **Category:** Custom Software Development • Digital Product Design • Business Systems

## 2. Brand purpose
Custom Technology & Software Development exists to help businesses and organizations move beyond generic, one-size-fits-all software. We design and develop digital systems specifically around the way each client operates.

That includes:
- custom websites and mobile applications
- customer, client, member and student/school portals and systems
- custom CRM platforms and third-party CRM integrations
- admin dashboards
- payment, booking and ecommerce systems
- membership platforms
- internal business tools, workflow automation and API integrations
- custom backend systems

The goal is not simply to create attractive websites. The goal is to build complete digital environments that help organizations operate more efficiently.

## 3. Positioning
We are not just a web-design company. CTSD is a **custom technology partner**. A website might be the public-facing part of a project; the larger value comes from what happens behind it:

Website → Customer registration → CRM → Client portal → Payments → Automated notifications → Admin dashboard → Reporting. CTSD can build the entire system.

**Positioning statement:** Custom Technology & Software Development creates websites, applications and digital systems designed around the unique operations of businesses and organizations.

## 4. Core promise
**BUILT AROUND YOU.** Businesses should not have to redesign their workflow simply because their software cannot accommodate it. We build technology around the organization. Not the other way around.

## 5. Differentiators
1. **Fully Custom.** Every organization works differently. Our systems are designed around the client's actual workflow rather than forcing them into rigid templates.
2. **Front-End + Back-End.** We do not only create what customers see. We also build the systems operating behind the experience: databases, admin portals, automation, permissions, integrations, CRM, analytics, payments, communication systems.
3. **Easy Client Management.** *Powerful technology without constant developer dependency.* Clients receive an easy-to-use administration system for routine operations:
   - update pages, change images
   - create events, manage users and memberships
   - add products, edit services
   - review submissions, manage bookings
   - send notifications, view reports

   Clients should not need to contact a developer every time they need to make a basic change.
4. **Connected Systems.** Instead of multiple disconnected services, one connected digital ecosystem: Website + App + CRM + Portal + Payments + Automation.
5. **Flexible Integrations.** Where building something custom does not make sense, we integrate established platforms: Salesforce, HubSpot, Stripe, PayPal, Square, Planning Center, Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, QuickBooks, Twilio, Mailchimp, Resend, Zapier, Make, Slack, Calendly, YouTube, Vimeo, Cloudflare.

## 13–20. Visual (colors and fonts superseded by the brand board)
- **Feel:** premium SaaS company + digital product studio, not a traditional IT consulting firm.
- **Logo:** CTSD, with the full name beneath or beside it. An abstract geometric C+T symbol. No computer, globe, Wi-Fi, code-bracket, gear or cursor icons.
- **UI style:**
  - generous spacing, large typography
  - rounded cards, subtle shadows, minimal borders
  - modern dashboards with clean charts and realistic data
  - simple icons, limited accent colors
  - no cluttered "impressive" dashboards; every interface should look usable
- **Photography:** no generic stock. Avoid people pointing at laptops, handshakes, random programmers, headsets, server rooms and fake meetings. **The product is the hero.**
- **Visual style:** websites in browser frames, apps on phones, CRM dashboards, portals, admin platforms, tablets and laptops, floating UI elements, integration and workflow diagrams, realistic business interfaces. *Show clients what they could own.*

## 32–34. Industry pages, process, services
- **Industry pages:** Business Technology, Church Technology, Education Technology, Nonprofit Technology.
- **Process:**
  1. **Discover:** We learn how the organization operates.
  2. **Design:** We design the customer experience and internal system.
  3. **Build:** Frontend, backend, integrations and infrastructure.
  4. **Test:** Devices, workflows, security and performance.
  5. **Launch:** Deployment and production setup.
  6. **Handoff:** Training and administration access.

  - **Digital Experience:** Websites, Mobile Applications, Ecommerce.
  - **Business Software:** CRM, Portals, Dashboards, Booking, Membership, Operations.
  - **Connected Systems:** API Integrations, Third-Party CRM, Payments, Automation, Authentication, Cloud Systems.
