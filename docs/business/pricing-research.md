# CTSD pricing research, October 2026

**Conclusion.** CTSD is priced below every Charlottesville studio entry price and at the bottom of national freelancer ranges. The prices used in `CTSD-Packages.pdf` are below.

Most published prices come from sellers' own pages, so treat them as marketing figures, not neutral benchmarks. Every figure here was read on the source page on 2026-10-22.

### National benchmarks

- **Freelancers.** Template customization $500–$1,500; custom 5–10 pages $1,500–$5,000; $30–$150/hour ([we.inc](https://we.inc/blog/website-design-cost)).
- **Agencies.** Small agencies $5,000–$15,000; agency rates $100–$250/hour (same source).
- **Basic packages.** These start at about $500 ([Thumbtack](https://www.thumbtack.com/va/charlottesville/web-design)).
- **Church websites.** Freelancers $500–$3,000; agencies from $4,500; most small and mid-size churches spend $500–$3,000 in year one ([By Crawford](https://bycrawford.com/blog/cost-to-build-a-church-website)).
- **Maintenance tiers.** Budget $25–$50/month; professional $75–$150; business $200–$400; enterprise $500–$600+ ([FatLab](https://fatlabwebsupport.com/blog/website-maintenance/website-maintenance-packages-compared-your-complete-buyers-guide/)).
- **Custom CRM.** Starter builds (contacts, pipeline, tasks) $20,000–$30,000, even at offshore rates ([QBS Global](https://qbsglobal.blog/cost-to-build-a-custom-crm-2026)).
- **Mobile apps.**
  - Simple apps free–$20,000; mid-level $20,000–$75,000; custom cross-platform $30,000–$150,000 ([GoDaddy](https://www.godaddy.com/resources/skills/how-much-does-it-cost-to-make-an-app)).
  - Simple apps $5,000–$25,000 ([Kellton](https://www.kellton.com/kellton-tech-blog/mobile-app-development-costs)).

## Risks to watch

- **Margin on packages 3 and 4.** The dashboard and Complete prices only work because the build reuses CTSD's own back office and starter template. Quote anything beyond the listed features as an add-on or a change request.
- **The $500 Starter Website** is profitable only at 1–3 pages from a starter design, with one round of changes. Hold that scope firmly.
- **The $90–$130/hour figure for Virginia designers** appeared in a search summary, but its source survey could not be found. It is not used here.
