# Basecamp Students: photo and video prompts

Generate these in Higgsfield with **Nano Banana**, save them to `assets-src/basecamp/` with the file names
below (PNG or JPG is fine), then run `node scripts/showcase-assets.mjs basecamp`. Each one replaces a
placeholder cropped from the concept images.

**Shared style (add to every prompt):** *candid documentary photo of a church youth group, teenagers aged
12–18, natural and unposed, warm practical light, slight film grain, no text, no logos, no brand marks on
clothing or shoes.* For any photo with faces, keep everyone clearly a teenager and in modest, everyday clothes.
